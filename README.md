# 🚨 Survival Hub

### Smart Assistance for Everyone

Survival Hub is a web-based platform designed to bring essential assistance and information together in one simple place.

It provides access to resources related to:

- 🍛 Food assistance
- 💼 Job opportunities
- 🏥 Health and hospitals
- 🏛️ Government schemes
- 📍 Nearby essential services
- 👤 User profile

---

## ✨ Features

### 🍛 Food Assistance
Users can view available food centers along with location and contact information.

### 💼 Jobs
Users can browse available job opportunities and search based on job title, company, or location.

### 🏥 Health
Users can view hospitals and medical assistance information with location support.

### 🏛️ Government Schemes
Users can explore available government schemes along with eligibility and application-related information.

### 📍 Nearby Services
Provides quick access to nearby essential services such as food centers, hospitals, employment offices, and MeeSeva centers.

### 👤 Profile
Users can save their name, phone number, and location.

### 🚨 Emergency
Provides quick access to the emergency helpline.

### 📱 Responsive Design
The website is designed to work across desktop and mobile screen sizes.
